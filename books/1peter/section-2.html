If the letter is inauthentic, it was likely written between 70 and 90 C.E., as argued by Elliott and Brown above. But some scholars such as W. G. Kmmel suggest the end of Domitian&#39;s reign (90-96 C.E.), contemporary with Revelation&#39;s supposed date (a time of persecution), while others such as N. Perrin believe it was written under Trajan (because Pliny&#39;s letter attests to persecution against Christians in Asia Minor then). In reply, the majority of scholars contend that the Christians being addressed were not enduring widespread persecution under Roman authority but rather harassment and ostracization by their neighbors.
