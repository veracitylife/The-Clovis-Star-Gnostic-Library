The Kolbrin Bible

The Kolbrin presents itself as a compilation of Egyptian and Celtic writings, blending mythic history, moral teaching, and spiritual reflection. Accounts of floods and cosmic upheaval sit alongside guidance on ethics and personal conduct. Some readers view it as an ancient remnant; others consider it a modern compilation influenced by esoteric traditions.
