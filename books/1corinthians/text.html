Recommended Books for the Study of Early Christian Writings Information on 1 Corinthians First Corinthians is one of the four letters of Paul known as the Hauptbriefe, which are universally accepted to be authentic.

The letter is usually dated c. 54/55 CE. Werner Georg Kummel states (Introduction to the New Testament, p. 275): &quot;The genuineness of I Cor is not disputed: the letter is already clearly known in I Clem 37:5; 47:1-3; 49:5; Ign., Eph 16:1; 18:1; Rom 5:1; Phila 3:3.&quot; Some Contemporary Texts Lost Sayings Gospel Q (40-80 A.D.) 1 Thessalonians (50-60 A.D.)
