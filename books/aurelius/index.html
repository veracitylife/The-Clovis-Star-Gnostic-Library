Recommended Books for the Study of Early Christian Writings Information on Marcus Aurelius Marcus Aurelius writes (Meditations 11.3): What a soul that is which is ready, if at any moment it must be separated from the body, and ready either to be extinguished or dispersed or continue to exist; but so that this readiness comes from a man&#39;s own judgement, not from mere obstinacy, as with the Christians, but considerately and with dignity and in a way to persuade another, without tragic show.
